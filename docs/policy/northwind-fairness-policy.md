# Northwind Support — Fairness and Access Policy

Northwind classifies inbound support tickets. This policy covers the model that does it.

## Data provenance

Data sources are listed in /policies/support-intake. We do not train on ticket bodies
written by customers.

## Retention

We retain ticket transcripts for 400 days. Retention period for audit logs is 400 days.

## Human oversight

Human review is available on request for any classification a customer disputes.
We review monthly samples for consistency.

## Consent and redress

An appeal is available for 60 days. Consent is required before a transcript is used for
training.

## Accountability

An audit trail of every classification is available to the internal audit team.

## Fairness

We monitor subgroup disparity across language, region and account age.

## Transparency

Interpretability is not currently available to customers; we document this limitation.
